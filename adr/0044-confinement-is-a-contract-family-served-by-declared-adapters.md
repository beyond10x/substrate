# ADR 0044: confinement is a contract family served by declared adapters

Decides `story:confinement-domain-specification`. It records in Substrate what atlas ADR 0109
(`architecture/adr/0109-confinement-is-one-substrate-contract-with-adapters.md` in the atlas
repository) decided for the collection. It writes the contract as the ESS domain
`substrate.confinement` before any adapter code, as invariant 8 requires.

## Acceptance

The operator accepted atlas ADR 0109 on 2026-10-27, each fork as recommended (F1 B, F2 A, F3 A).
The same day the operator accepted the plan that implements it, `epic:confinement-contract-family`
and its stories. This story is one of them, and it names this ADR and its title.

## Context

Substrate confines a process today through one Linux driver: bubblewrap, namespaces, cgroup v2,
seccomp and `openat2` behind `HostDriver` in `b10x-substrate-host`. ADR 0037 makes the macOS
platform sandbox (Seatbelt) the confinement on macOS, with its own capability facts and no limits
or whole-tree kill. Outside Substrate, other repositories in the collection confine processes with
their own code: Docker, `systemd-run`, Seatbelt and `unshare`. Atlas ADR 0109 moves all of it into
Substrate. It names six adapters: `local`, `linux`, `macos`, `systemd`, `oci` and `pod`. Each adapter
serves a subset of a family of contracts (decision 8), and every adapter is specified in ESS
(decision 7).

## Decision

1. **Confinement is a family of contracts.** The families are `process_run`, `process_watch`,
   `output_read`, `wait`, `workspace`, `filesystem_view`, `network_egress`, `network_ingress`,
   `limits` and `audit`, in that order (atlas ADR 0109 decision 8). An adapter declares the families
   it serves and the facts it proved when it opened. A profile names the families and facts it
   needs.
2. **An adapter is opened, then profiles are admitted on it.** A profile that needs a family the
   opened adapter does not serve is refused `confinement.family-unserved` (501), naming the first
   such family in the order above. A profile that needs a fact the adapter did not prove is refused
   `confinement.fact-unserved` (501), naming the fact. An adapter that cannot open on this host is
   `confinement.adapter-unserved` (501). Nothing degrades silently (invariant 3).
3. **`local` is declared insecure.** It needs a reason (`confinement.local-reason-missing`, 422),
   and its admission records `declared_insecure: true` with that reason. A host refuses it through
   a policy fact (`confinement.local-refused`, 403; atlas ADR 0109 F3 A). `systemd` around `local`
   bounds the run and leaves it declared insecure.
4. **`oci` and `pod` run an image by digest only.** An image named by tag is
   `confinement.image-unpinned` (422).
5. **Ingress routes and rate bounds are reserved.** Setting either is
   `confinement.reserved-field-unserved` (501) until a later story fills it (atlas ADR 0109 F1 B).
6. **The contract is the ESS domain `substrate.confinement`** in `spec/confinement/`. Every adapter's
   scenarios are authored there, each named for the lane that runs it (`-on-portable`,
   `-on-delegated`, `-on-macos`, `-on-oci`, `-on-pod`). `cargo xtask check-ess` holds the committed
   suite `spec/confinement-suite.json` and the generated `ContractFamily`, `AdapterName` and `Fact`
   in `crates/substrate-host/src/generated/confinement.rs` to their regenerated bytes, and runs the
   suite against the ESS `interpreted` reference. That run checks the model. Each adapter story adds
   a test file that runs its lane against the adapter, and no adapter story edits the suite.
7. **The contract is substrate-host's API, not the wire.** No route, wire field or contract bundle
   changes. `macos` keeps ADR 0037's plan: its wire facts arrive in a successor bundle in their own
   story.
8. **The `oci` and `pod` adapters serve the confinement contract through substrate-host's API.**
   They are not the wire-level drivers of plan 03 (`docs/plan/03-container-driver.md`): not the
   Docker driver of `docs/design/15-docker-driver-entry-gate.md`, which stays deferred, and not a
   Kubernetes namespace driver. A client that reads which adapter answered is choosing an adapter
   in substrate-host's API. Through the daemon it still meets one driver contract (invariant 4).

## Consequences

- `crates/substrate-host` gains the confinement contract's module in
  `story:confinement-contract-on-linux-and-local`. Until then the generated types are private.
- Open questions are `UNMAPPED:` markers in `spec/confinement/confinement.yaml`, listed in
  `spec/confinement/README.md`:
  - whether `systemd` without `inner`, or with an `inner` other than the opened one, is a refusal;
  - the refusal's audit record, which ESS cannot attach to an error outcome;
  - an image beside an adapter that runs no image;
  - the shape of the reserved fields;
  - facts that no story declares for an adapter.
  Each is decided in the story that meets it, by a successor scenario and, where it changes this
  decision, a successor ADR.
- A wire publication of adapters or facts is a `HostDriverKind` change and needs a successor bundle
  (invariant 6). Atlas ADR 0109 names that as a later step.

## Considered

| | option | why not |
|---|---|---|
| A | a new wire driver per mechanism (plan 03's shape) | a client would branch on the driver, and every adapter would cost a bundle; atlas ADR 0109 puts the adapters behind one host API |
| B | one confinement library outside Substrate | atlas ADR 0109 Considered A and C: it breaks invariant 2, or a second version line must agree on the facts |
