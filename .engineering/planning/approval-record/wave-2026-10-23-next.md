## Decisions taken at dispatch

- u2 keeps the observation `unknown` after the retry, whether or not the retry proves the tree
  empty. That changes no recorded observation, so invariant 8 needs no design note. Promoting a
  proven-empty retry to a terminal state is left to a later story.
- u3 names the capability fact `exec.read-only-roots`, following `exec.read-only-files`
  (bundle `0.18.0`), and cuts bundle `0.23.0`. ADR 0010
  (`adr/0010-declared-host-roots-are-mounted-read-only.md:79`) already decides that the bundle and
  the capability snapshot carry the field.
