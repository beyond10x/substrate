needs-revision

epic:confinement-contract-family — the Delivery section promises "the epic ships as one pull request and one release", but the operator's quote in the same section asks only for "1 single PR in the end", and no story or "Done when" line claims a release. Drop "and one release", or exclude it explicitly (a release is its own request, with the `Confinement-lane:` and `Quota-lane:` tag records) — .engineering/planning/epic/confinement-contract-family.md:77

story:oci-adapter — it and story:sandbox-repository-parity both claim that the `oci` `render` output contains the `sandbox` Docker flags. oci: "a portable test asserts it holds every flag listed above". parity: "asserts each is present in the `oci` adapter's `render` output". Narrow the oci assertion to the flags `sandbox` lacks (`--pids-limit`, `--memory`/`--memory-swap`, `--cpus`, digest) and leave the `sandbox` flags to parity — .engineering/planning/story/oci-adapter.md:57-58 and .engineering/planning/story/sandbox-repository-parity.md:55

story:ess-pin-0-70-0 — the story promises "the ESS pin moves to 0.70.0", but its acceptance and scope cover only the `requires: ess` lines and `spec/README.md`. Four other places name 0.69.2 and fall outside both. `xtask/src/ess.rs:19` is the install error message. `spec/coverage.md:93,152,157`, `spec/host-processes/README.md:27` and `spec/workspaces/README.md:28,68` state facts "under ESS 0.69.2". The story should require that no `0.69.2` string remains under `spec/`, `xtask/` and `.github/`, and add those files to its scope — `grep -rn "0\.69\.2" spec xtask .github` and .engineering/planning/story/ess-pin-0-70-0.md:51

**What I read:** 11 artifacts plus ADR 0109 in full, via `aep plan artifact show` on each and on `review-result:adr-0109-plan-scope-round-1`, with `gh release list -R beyond10x/ess` and `grep` over `spec/`, `xtask/` and `crates/substrate-wire/src/generated`. I extracted 27 promises from the ADR, the epic and the operator's order, and traced 25 to a story or a named exclusion. The two untraced are the epic's own "one release" and the 0.69.2 strings the pin story misses. All six round-1 findings are fixed:
- fact-unserved refusal and required facts: in the domain story
- per-adapter scenario floor: in the domain story
- IPC: in the S0 profile
- `agent-stack/stack.yaml` node: named in the epic's Out of scope
- ESS pin: its own story
- consumer surface: narrowed

I did not run `graph`, `kinds` or `relations` this round, and used the relations printed by each `show`.

**What I could not establish:**
- `approval-record:adr-0109-confinement-plan` is created after this round, so I could not read the operator quote it carries.
- I did not run ESS, so I cannot say whether 0.70.0 changes the generated Rust bodies beyond the `@generated by ESS 0.69.2` header lines.
- Out of lane for design: the epic's bullet about plan 03's Docker and Kubernetes drivers sits under `## Delivery`, below the Out of scope list.
- Out of lane for design: the S2 macos story changes the `macos` facts, but the committed suite is "never edited" by adapter stories. A scenario asserting `sealed_paths` is refused on `macos` would fail after S2.
- Out of lane for acceptance:
  - The domain floor requires an `adapter-unserved` scenario "for an absent platform or runtime" for `local`, which has no such state.
  - The consumer-surface test needs a working directory and an install-root writable root. The S0 profile list names neither (it lists "workspaces (named writable subtrees)").
