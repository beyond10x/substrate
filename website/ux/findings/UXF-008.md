The Docusaurus port of 2026-10-27 dropped the underline the earlier stylesheet gave links in
article text. The theme's link colour does not reach 3:1 against the text in either theme, so a
link is told from its sentence by colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1). Fix: links in article text are
underlined.
